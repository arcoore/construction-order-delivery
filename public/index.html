FAQS

Straight answers.

What does SiteStock do?+

SiteStock helps construction teams keep material requests, approvals, purchasing and delivery updates organised in one shared workflow across people and sites.

Does SiteStock buy materials for us?+

No. The current workflow helps your Buyer coordinate and record purchasing. A “Purchased” status records progress in SiteStock; it does not mean SiteStock placed or paid for a merchant order.

Do we still need a Buyer?+

The current workflow includes a Buyer. Reducing manual buying work is a longer-term ambition, not a capability available today.

Does every request need Owner approval?+

No. Approval is configurable at Company level. When it is switched off, eligible requests can proceed to the Buyer without that stage.

Are supplier prices and stock live?+

Not currently. Live supplier pricing and stock integrations are deferred. Any values shown in early product examples are demo data, not live merchant information.

Does SiteStock include live driver tracking?+

No. Delivery progress is based on recorded status updates. SiteStock does not currently claim live GPS tracking or route optimisation.
